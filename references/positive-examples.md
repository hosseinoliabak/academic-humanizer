## 1. State the research setting directly

**Author-selected positive example**

> The main research setting consists of separate organizations or service-provider customers.

**Supplied feedback:** Medium Human impact; Factual Clarity. The explanation praises the clear,
unadorned presentation and describes the structure as basic subject-verb-object.

**Editing observation:** The sentence states the setting directly. Preserve that useful simplicity;
do not add an introductory flourish or replace ordinary wording solely to vary its grammar. Treat
the supplied grammatical description as feedback, not an authoritative parse or a sentence template.

**Default action:** Retain the sentence when it fits the paragraph and the stated setting is supported.
Review adjacent sentences for flow before making a local change.

**Preservation check:** Keep "main," "separate," and the alternative expressed by "or." Retain the
distinction between organizations and service-provider customers. Do not change the alternatives to
"and," equate the two groups, or infer a particular federation or deployment topology.

**Evidence status:** The research setting is supplied manuscript content. Selection as a positive
style example does not independently verify the study's actual participants or design.

## 2. Introduce the literature in a neutral academic voice

**Author-selected positive example**

> Network intrusion detection already has a substantial collaborative-learning literature.

**Supplied feedback:** Journalistic Style; Factual Clarity. The explanation praises neutral,
informative reporting and a direct, simple structure without embellishment or figurative language.

**Editing observation:** Keep the direct topic introduction and precise field name. Here the
"Journalistic Style" label supports neutral reporting within an academic register; it does not
require conversational wording, a news lead, or an anecdote.

**Default action:** Retain this presentation when it fits the literature-review argument. Do not
inflate "has" into "has witnessed remarkable growth" or add generic importance claims. Do not
replace collaborative learning with federated learning unless the manuscript and sources support
that narrower scope.

**Preservation check:** Keep network intrusion detection as the field and collaborative learning as
the stated literature scope. "Already" carries a temporal emphasis, and "substantial" assesses the
extent of the literature. Do not treat either word as filler or as independently verified evidence.

**Evidence status:** This example approves a presentation style. The literature claim still needs
support from the review or cited sources when used as a factual assertion. Finding a related study
does not by itself verify the size of the literature. Do not invent references or findings to make
the sentence appear supported.
